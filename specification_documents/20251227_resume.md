\begin{rSection}{Research Experience}
{\bf {Multi-agent System for the Analog Code Generation}}
\\Proposing \textbf{MenTeR}, a novel multi-agent AI workflow that automates and accelerates end-to-end RF/analog circuit design by reducing trial-and-error and expanding design space exploration, laying the foundation for future AI-assisted analog design tools. (Pin-Han Chen*, \textbf{Yu-Sheng Lin*}, Wei-Cheng Lee*, Tin-Yu Leu, Po-Hsiang Hsu, Anjana Dissanayake, Sungjin Oh, Chinq-Shiun Chiu." MenTeR: A fully-automated Multi-agenT workflow for end-to-end RF/Analog Circuits Netlist Design." Submitted to IEEE International Conference on LLM-Aided Design (\MYhref{https://iclad.ai/}{IEEE ICLAD}). 2025. \MYhref{https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22990}{[paper]})\\

{\bf {Physics Informed Neural Network (PINN) for thermal simulation}}
\\Proposed a U-Net trained with Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) loss for System-on-Chip (SoC) thermal simulation. Incorporated the Sobel operator into the loss function to enforce adherence to the governing thermal equation (Fourier-Biot) during training. (\textbf{Yu-Sheng Lin*}, Li-Song Lin*, Chin-Jui Chang*, Ting-Yu Lin, Shih-Hong Pan, Ya-Wen Yu, Kai-En Yang, Wei Cheng Lee, Yi-Chen Lin, Tai-Yu Chen, Jason Yeh." Fast SoC thermal simulation with physics-aware U-Net." Workshop at the 37th conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS). 2023. \MYhref{https://ml4physicalsciences.github.io/2023/files/NeurIPS_ML4PS_2023_13.pdf}{[paper]})\\


{\bf {Explainable AI (XAI) on Face Recognition}}
\\Proposing a novel explainable metric, called $xCos$, that provides spatial explanation for the face verification task. (\textbf{Yu-Sheng Lin}, Zhe-Yu Liu, Yu-An Chen, Yu-Siang Wang, Ya-Liang Chang, Winston H. Hsu. "xCos: An Explainable Cosine Metric for Face Verification Task." ACM Transactions on Multimedia Computing Communications and Applications (TOMM). 2021. 
\MYhref{https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05383}{[paper]}
\MYhref{https://eccv2020.eu/demonstrations/}{[ECCV 2020 Demo]} \MYhref{https://youtu.be/lFIW_w0y08A}{[Demo Video]} \MYhref{https://github.com/ntubiolin/xcos}{[GitHub]})\\

{\bf {Object Detection under Extreme Low-Light Conditions with RAW Images}}
\\Proposing a high-quality large-scale Night Object Detection (NOD) dataset showing dynamic scenes captured on the streets at night. Improve generalization to unseen camera sensors by implementing a minimal neural ISP pipeline for machine cognition, named GenISP.

Igor Morawski, Yu-An Chen, \textbf{Yu-Sheng Lin}, Winston H. Hsu. "NOD: Taking a Closer Look at Detection under Extreme Low-Light Conditions with Night Object Detection Dataset." The British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC). 2021.
\MYhref{https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.10364}{[paper]}
\MYhref{https://forms.gle/YhHYBofVjphosbeDA}{[Dataset]} \MYhref{https://github.com/igor-morawski/nod}{[GitHub]}

\MYhref{https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03688}{[paper]}
